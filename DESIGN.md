# Design System: Mohanad Yehia Software QA Portfolio

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Object Under Test"**

One live object stands for the system under test. On the homepage it is a real-time WebGL stage of dark chrome and amber glass, fixed behind the document; the cursor inspects it and scrolling turns it into each body of evidence. Everything written sits in HTML in front of it, on graphite, in warm white. The reading pages (work archive, case studies, not found) keep the same chrome, heading voice, and closing without the stage, and carry a still plate of the object beside their opening.

The system is dark from end to end. Every chapter stays on graphite; the owner withdrew a paper-coloured chapter and an amber-coloured chapter, so there is no light or saturated field anywhere. Amber is an accent: the emphasised word in a heading, the Blue Ribbon figures, thin rules, hover and current states, and the primary action. Type carries the hierarchy with two sans families and one serif italic used as a second headline voice. Surfaces are square and flat, separated by one-pixel rules; depth belongs to the object.

The homepage holds (pins) its chapters on wide screens while the stage fits, and flows on narrow screens. Phones show a still until the first drawn frame. Reduced motion, no JavaScript, and no WebGL2 keep the full page with the still.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Graphite field on every page and chapter, warm white type, amber as accent only.
- Alexandria for display, Manrope for text, Bodoni Moda italic for one emphasised word or phrase per heading and for the Blue Ribbon figures.
- One chapter scale for chapter titles; only the hero, project titles, and the closing are larger.
- Square, flat DOM surfaces separated by one-pixel warm rules. The two rounded shapes are the action corner and the circular lens.
- A fixed live stage behind the homepage document; a still plate of the object on reading pages.
- Twelve-column chapters with copy on one side and the object or the evidence on the other.
- Complete content without motion, scripts, or WebGL2.

The legacy study page `src/pages/experience.astro` (with `BaseLayout.astro`, `Header.astro`, `Contact.astro`, and `PassThrough.astro`) is still on the previous layout and is not part of this system.

## Colors

A warm mineral dark: one graphite ground, three warm neutrals for text, one amber accent, and a soft red reserved for recorded failures.

### Primary

- **Amber** (`colors.amber`): the emphasised serif word in headings, the Blue Ribbon figures, result figures, descriptor lines under project and framework titles, current navigation, hover on links and row titles, the focus outline, selection, the reading progress line, short list rules, and the Resume button in the chrome.
- **Bright Amber** (`colors.amber-bright`): the hover fill of both amber buttons (the primary action and the chrome Resume button).

### Neutral

- **Graphite** (`colors.graphite`): the page ground on every page and chapter, the menu overlay, the loader, the solid chrome on reading pages, and the stage's base tone.
- **Warm White** (`colors.ink`): headings, default text, primary links, and strong terms. At ten percent opacity it sets the oversized framework names in the archive reel.
- **Reading Stone** (`colors.body-copy`): sustained description and case-study prose.
- **Muted Stone** (`colors.muted`): metadata, captions, scope notes, periods, table headers, and quiet links.
- **Warm Rule** (`colors.line`): every one-pixel divider, the evidence panel border, tracks behind amber fills, and the hairline under the solid chrome.
- **Raised Graphite** (`colors.surface`): the single raised field, used by the case-study evidence panel.
- **Amber Ink** (`colors.paper-ink`): dark text on flat amber fills (Resume button, skip link, selection). The source variable is still named `--paper-ink` from the withdrawn paper field; there is no paper surface.

### Status and stage

- **Recorded Failure** (`colors.fail`): failed runs in the TestPulse history, the retained accessibility failures in the Cal.diy audit, and that panel's top rule and emphasised word. Always paired with a word ("Failed", "Two failures retained") and, in the history, a cross icon.

### Named Rules

**The Graphite Throughout Rule.** Every chapter and every page sits on Graphite. No light field and no saturated field. The only tonal lift is Raised Graphite on the evidence panel.

**The Amber Is An Accent Rule.** Amber marks emphasis, figures, rules, state, and the primary action. It never becomes a section ground.

**The Words With The Colour Rule.** A result is never carried by colour alone. Recorded Failure always arrives with its word and scope.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Alexandria Variable (with sans-serif)
**Body Font:** Manrope Variable (with sans-serif)
**Second Headline Voice:** Bodoni Moda Variable italic (with Bodoni 72, Didot, serif)
**Code Font:** ui-monospace (with SF Mono, Menlo, Consolas, monospace), inline code only

**Character:** A wide, tightly tracked geometric sans states the claim; a high-contrast serif italic in amber picks out the one word that matters. Manrope keeps everything else plain and legible.

All headings (`h1` to `h3`) default to Alexandria at weight 600, line height 1.05, letter spacing -0.04em, balanced wrapping. Sizes are set per role and are fluid; there is no single modular ratio.

### The serif voice

`typography.serif-voice` is always italic, weight 500, optical sizing on, in Amber. It applies to `em` inside `h1` to `h3` and takes the heading's size (1.05em in the hero and page titles, 1.04em in the layers sentence). The Blue Ribbon figures use it at weight 600 and 1.2em with lining numerals; their plus and percent signs are set back in Alexandria at 0.62em because the serif's signs are hairlines. The not-found numeral uses it once at display size.

### Named Rules

**The One Chapter Scale Rule.** Chapter titles use `typography.chapter`. Only the hero, the project titles, and the closing are larger. The owner rejected oversized chapter type.

**The One Serif Phrase Rule.** Bodoni Moda italic marks one emphasised word or phrase in a heading, and the Blue Ribbon figures. It never sets running text; emphasis inside paragraphs stays ordinary.

**The Tabular Figures Rule.** Tables, definition values, run identifiers, periods, counts, and the clock use tabular numerals.

## Layout

**Page frame.** Content runs edge to edge inside one gutter (`spacing.page-gutter`; `spacing.page-gutter-compact` at 600px and below). There is no centred max-width container; measure is held per text block in `ch` (16ch claims, 26ch to 46ch descriptions, 62ch list descriptions, a 41rem prose column).

**Chrome.** A transparent bar of `spacing.chrome-height` (`spacing.chrome-height-compact` below 1024px) sits over the first screen. Chapters that fill the screen start their padding at the chrome height.

**Homepage.** The stage is fixed behind the document at the lowest layer; the document scrolls in front of it. Chapters are at least one screen tall (`100svh`). Order: hero, layers, three projects, framework archive, experience, approach, about, closing.

- Project chapters use a twelve-column grid with `spacing.column-gap`: copy spans six columns, evidence five, and the side alternates left, right, left so the object takes the other half.
- Experience has two parts. First a held stage: the chapter title and the figures sentence on the left (up to 54 percent of the width) beside the object as a column of 22 slabs. Then the two roles on flat graphite, in a five-column head and six-column body, while the stage rests behind a graphite gradient that fades over `spacing.dissolve` at top and bottom.
- Approach keeps its copy to the right half (up to 52 percent) with the object on the left. Four steps, one in focus at a time; the others stay fully readable.
- About is a two-column grid (1 to 1.3) with the portrait in a 4 by 5 frame.
- The closing is a full screen on every page: the title in two lines with the second indented, a note and the email address, then a ruled foot.

**Reading pages.** The opening is a two-column grid (1.15 to 1) with copy on the left and a still plate of the object on the right in a 3 by 2 field, at least 86svh tall. Case studies follow with the evidence panel, then a 17rem sticky sidebar beside a 41rem prose column. Lists are ruled rows on the twelve-column grid. Sections that are not full-screen use `spacing.section-block` vertically.

**Rhythm.** Vertical spacing is fluid (`clamp`) and tied to viewport width. Ruled rows pad between 1.4rem and `clamp(2rem, 3.4vw, 3.5rem)`.

**Responsive behaviour.**

- Below 1024px, and on coarse pointers for the stage: chapters flow instead of holding, grids collapse to one column, the object takes the upper part of the screen (copy starts at 46svh, the closing at 40svh), the chrome swaps navigation and Resume for the menu button, and compact type roles apply.
- Below 900px: reading-page openings and the case reading grid collapse to one column; the sidebar stops sticking.
- Below 760px: reading-page compact type; framework rows and earlier builds stack.
- At 600px and below: compact gutter; the history stacks into rows; the strategy table becomes a definition list.

**Motion.** Scroll drives the stage and the held chapters with linear scrubbing. Entrances and state changes ease out on one curve (`cubic-bezier(.16, 1, .3, 1)`). Colour state changes take 0.2s to 0.25s; position and reveal take 0.4s to 0.8s. Page changes use a view transition in which the project title travels between the homepage, the archive, and its case study. Reduced motion removes scroll smoothing, transitions, and the view transition.

**The Held Or Flowing Rule.** A chapter holds only on wide screens with motion allowed and the stage running. Everywhere else it flows as ordinary document content, with nothing hidden.

**The Complete Still Rule.** The full page reads with the still image of the object when motion is reduced, scripts are off, or WebGL2 is missing. Phones show the still until the stage has drawn its first frame.

## Elevation & Depth

DOM surfaces are flat. No drop shadows are used. Depth comes from three things: the live stage behind the document, the dissolve gradient that lets graphite cover and uncover the stage around the roles list, and a single tonal lift (Raised Graphite with a one-pixel Warm Rule border) for the evidence panel.

### Shadow Vocabulary

- **Chrome hairline** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 0 #49433b`, the Warm Rule): appears under the chrome only when it returns as a solid bar over reading-page content. It is a rule, not a shadow.

### Layer order

Stage and still at the bottom (0), document content (1), experience and approach chapters (2), menu overlay (9), chrome (10), reading progress line (25), loader (30), skip link (40).

### Named Rules

**The Depth Belongs To The Object Rule.** Glass, chrome, glow, and bloom live in the stage and in its still plates. HTML surfaces stay flat and are separated by rules and tone.

## Shapes

Square by default (`rounded.square`): panels, rows, tables, the portrait frame, and bars have no radius. Two shapes are rounded. Actions take a soft corner (`rounded.action`). The lens is a circle (`rounded.lens`): the hero hint ring, the ring that follows the cursor over the approach title, the dots in the archive reel, and the circular wipe that opens the menu.

Lines are one pixel in Warm Rule. Amber lines are one or two pixels: the two-pixel rule on top of the evidence panel, the two-pixel underline of the checked filter, the two-pixel reading progress line and agreement track, and the short one-pixel dash before each role highlight. Icons are 24-unit outline strokes at 1.5 width with round caps, drawn in the current text colour.

## Components

### Buttons

Plain, compact, and few. There are three.

- **Shape:** soft corner (`rounded.action`), minimum height 48px, horizontal padding 1.4rem, Manrope 600.
- **Primary** (`components.button-primary`): amber gradient fill with dark text. Used for the hero's main action.
- **Quiet** (`components.button-quiet`): one-pixel warm border over a translucent graphite fill, Warm White text. Used beside the primary action.
- **Resume** (`components.button-resume`): flat Amber with Amber Ink text, minimum height 44px, in the chrome on wide screens.
- **Hover / Focus:** amber buttons brighten to Bright Amber; the quiet button fills. Transitions are 0.2s on colour. Focus is a two-pixel Amber outline offset by 4px, on every interactive element.

### Text Links

- **Style** (`components.text-link`): Warm White, weight 600, a one-pixel underline in the current colour, minimum height 44px, an outline icon after the label.
- **Hover:** Amber; the icon moves 4px along its direction.
- **Quiet** (`components.text-link-quiet`): Muted Stone, for the secondary link in a pair.
- **Back link:** Muted Stone note text with a reversed arrow, above reading-page titles.

### Navigation

- **Chrome** (`components.navigation`): brand name at the left, five links and the Resume button at the right. Links are Warm White with a one-pixel underline that draws in from the left on hover and focus. The current section is Amber with the underline shown.
- **Behaviour:** absolute on first paint, fixed once scripts run. On reading pages it slides away while reading down, returns on the way up with a solid Graphite ground and the hairline, and always returns for keyboard focus.
- **Narrow screens:** links and Resume are replaced by a labelled Menu button with a two-line icon that crosses into a close mark. The menu is a full-screen Graphite overlay that opens with a circular wipe from the button; links are ruled, set in `typography.menu-link`, and enter in sequence.
- **Skip link** (`components.skip-link`): Amber, first in the tab order.

### Lead Rows

A row that leads somewhere (`components.lead-row`): the title carries it and the arrow answers the pointer. Ruled top and bottom in Warm Rule, title in `typography.row-title`, an Amber descriptor line under it. On hover and focus the title turns Amber and the arrow moves 0.5rem.

### Evidence Panel

- **Corner Style:** square.
- **Background:** Raised Graphite (`components.evidence-panel`).
- **Border:** one-pixel Warm Rule, with a two-pixel Amber rule across the top that draws in from the left. The Cal.diy panel uses Recorded Failure for that rule and for its emphasised word.
- **Shadow Strategy:** none.
- **Internal Padding:** fluid, 1.5rem to 4rem.
- **Contents:** an evidence headline, then a ruled table with a caption, or three result figures, then a scope note in Muted Stone. Retained failures are bold in Recorded Failure and say so in words.

### Homepage Evidence Figures

Each project chapter carries one small figure with a caption that states the scope.

- **History:** three ruled runs, each with a check or cross icon, the word Passed or Failed, the run identifier, and the time.
- **Contention:** twenty bars, one tall in Amber and nineteen short in warm grey.
- **Agreement:** one large Amber figure, its scope beside it, and a two-pixel track filled to the same value.

### Filters

- **Style** (`components.filter-option`): radio inputs presented as text options in Muted Stone with a count, minimum height 44px, no box.
- **Checked** (`components.filter-option-checked`): Amber text with a two-pixel Amber underline.
- **Focus:** two-pixel Amber outline on the label. The form appears only when scripts are available; the full list is the default.

### Ruled Lists

Roles, approach steps, framework rows, earlier builds, and profile facts are all rows between one-pixel rules: a heading or label at the left (five columns), the body at the right (six columns), stacking to one column on narrow screens.

### The Stage

A single WebGL scene fixed behind the homepage. Materials are dark chrome and amber glass on Graphite. It fades in over 1.1s once it has drawn, and drops to 28 percent opacity when the document releases it. The still plate stands in before the first frame on phones and permanently when no stage will arrive.

### Hero-only devices

The hand-drawn ring around the second emphasised word and the pulsing lens beside the hint belong to the hero and are not general components.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** keep every chapter and page on Graphite (`colors.graphite`) with Warm White text.
- **Do** use Amber for emphasis words, figures, thin rules, hover and current states, focus, and the primary action.
- **Do** set chapter titles at `typography.chapter`, weight 700, and reserve larger type for the hero, project titles, and the closing.
- **Do** mark one word or phrase per heading with the Bodoni Moda italic voice in Amber.
- **Do** separate content with one-pixel Warm Rule lines and keep panels square.
- **Do** give copy one side of a chapter and the object or the evidence the other.
- **Do** state the scope beside every figure, and pair Recorded Failure with a word.
- **Do** keep interactive targets at 44px or taller with the two-pixel Amber focus outline.
- **Do** keep the page complete with the still when motion is reduced, scripts are off, or WebGL2 is missing.

### Don't:

- **Don't** introduce a light or saturated chapter field. The owner withdrew the paper-coloured and the amber-coloured chapters.
- **Don't** oversize chapter titles. The owner rejected chapter type above the chapter scale.
- **Don't** set running text or paragraph emphasis in the serif italic.
- **Don't** add drop shadows or rounded panels to HTML surfaces.
- **Don't** carry a result by colour alone.
- **Don't** treat the legacy study page (`src/pages/experience.astro` and its layout) as a reference for new surfaces.
